How to play

What's the goal of a case?

Each case is a floor plan divided into rooms. Place every suspect on the grid so that each row and each column holds exactly one suspect, and every clue is true. When everyone is placed, the killer is the one suspect who was alone in the room with the victim. Then you make your accusation.

How do I place and mark suspects?

Pick a suspect from the tiles under the board, then tap a cell to place them. Switch on Mark to pencil an X in a cell you've ruled out. Tap it again to remove the mark. Undo steps back one move, and Clear empties the board.

How do I read the clues and testimony?

Select a suspect to see their clue card. Family members' testimony is always true, but they phrase it to protect themselves or blame someone else, so read exactly what is said, not what is implied.

Is every case solvable without guessing?

Yes. Every case has exactly one solution, checked by our puzzle engine, and can be solved by logic alone. If a case seems impossible, look for a clue about a room, a row or a neighbour that you haven't used yet, or take a hint.

Strikes and stars

What is a strike?

Placing a suspect in a spot that contradicts the solution costs a strike. The board flashes red and the placement is rejected. You have three strikes per case. The third strike fails the case, and the killer walks free. Accusing the wrong suspect also fails the case. You can always retry a failed case.

How do stars work?

Solved cases earn stars based on strikes: ★★★ with no strikes, ★★ with one, ★ with two. Replaying a case keeps your best result. The total on each pack card shows your stars out of 3 per case.

What is the Daily Case and the streak?

A new Daily Case appears every day, the same for every player. Solve it on consecutive days to grow your streak.

Hints

How do hints work?

Hints are always free. Tap Hint and the game reveals the next logical step. In the free version there's a short wait between hints. You can wait it out, or optionally watch a short ad to skip the wait. With the Detective's Licence, hints are unlimited and instant.

Purchases

What does the Detective's Licence include?

It's a one-time purchase ($3.99 in the US) that removes all ads and gives unlimited, instant hints, forever. It's not a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, open Settings & Store in the app, and tap Restore purchases. If Family Sharing isn't enabled, the purchase can only be restored on the Apple Account that bought it.

I was charged but ads still show.

Tap Restore purchases in Settings & Store first. If that doesn't help, make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase, and email us with the date of purchase. Do not send card details.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

How often are ads shown?

At most one ad after every third solved case, never in the middle of a case. Rewarded ads are only shown if you choose to watch one to skip the hint wait.

Why does the app ask to track?

iOS asks whether our ad provider (Google AdMob) may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. The game works exactly the same whether you allow it or not. You can change it any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Progress and troubleshooting

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Progress is stored only on your device, with no account. Deleting the app deletes your progress. It may come back if you restore the whole device from an iCloud or computer backup. Your Detective's Licence can always be restored.

The app is slow, frozen or crashed.

Force-quit the app (swipe it away in the app switcher) and reopen it, and make sure you have the latest version from the App Store. If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and iOS version.
