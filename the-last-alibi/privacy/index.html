The Last Alibi Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version. The Last Alibi has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your cases, stars, streaks and settings stay on your iPhone. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and ad data to serve and measure those ads. Ads are only personalized if you allow tracking when iOS asks. Purchases are handled by Apple.

This policy explains how MOBI MEDIA, INC. ("we", "us") handles information in The Last Alibi (the "App") for iPhone and iPad. By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any account login. The App has no sign-up, no chat and no social features, and we run no servers that receive your gameplay.

2. Game data stored only on your device

Your progress (solved and failed cases, stars, strikes, the daily-case streak, hint timers, how many cases since the last ad), your settings (sound, haptics), and whether you own the Detective's Licence are saved in a local database on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the App deletes it. Depending on your iOS settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the device, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads appear at most once after every third solved case, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to skip the free-hint wait. To deliver, cap, measure and protect those ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App may collect and send to Google:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location, such as country or city);
  • Identifiers, such as the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) or other app- or developer-scoped identifiers, and the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it (see below);
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were requested and shown, impressions, taps, video views and rewarded-ad completions;
  • Diagnostics and performance data, such as SDK crash logs, app launch time and ad load times.

App Tracking Transparency. iOS may ask whether you allow the App to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Allow, Google may use the IDFA and related data to show you ads that are more relevant to you and to measure ad performance. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared with Google, and you will still see ads, which may be less relevant (for example, based on the content of the App or your general location). The App works the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage ad personalization at Google My Ad Center.

If you buy the Detective's Licence, the App stops requesting ads.

4. In-app purchases (Apple)

The optional Detective's Licence ($3.99 in the US, one-time) is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details. The App only receives confirmation from Apple (StoreKit), on your device, that the purchase is owned, so it can remove ads, unlock unlimited hints, and restore the purchase on a new device. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. App updates (Expo)

The App can download small content and bug-fix updates through Expo's EAS Update service (650 Industries, Inc.). To decide which update to send, the App sends Expo its version, platform and operating system, a randomly generated installation ID that is not linked to you or your device hardware, and your IP address as part of the network request. Expo uses this only to deliver updates and count installs. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

6. Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. We use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

7. How information is used and shared

We do not sell personal information. We do not receive the data collected by Google or Expo in a form that identifies you; those providers process it to provide their services as described above. Beyond the providers above (Google, Apple and Expo), we share information only if the law requires it.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game with mild murder-mystery themes. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it, including by asking our ad provider to do so where possible.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: allow or deny it in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Turning off Allow Apps to Request to Track denies it for all apps.
  • Ads: buy the Detective's Licence to remove ads, and manage ad personalization in Google My Ad Center.
  • Game data: delete the App to erase all locally stored progress.
  • Your privacy rights: depending on where you live (for example California, the EEA or the UK), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best handled through the settings above or directly with Google. You can still contact us and we will help.

10. Security and retention

Game data lives on your device and is protected by iOS. We do not keep personal information on our own systems. Google, Apple and Expo keep the data they collect according to their own retention policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
